Lores of Magic

Which lores are actually played in PvP, and which spells to take in each one. Tiers are indicative: Meta is what you see in most games, Situational needs the right matchup or caster, Weak is rarely played.

Two spells is the usual pick: one expensive, one cheap to cast often.
Slows and debuffs that cannot be dodged are worth more than damage that can miss.
Overcast when it doubles the effect for less than twice the cost.
